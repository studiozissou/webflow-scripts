# Client Context — MRC (Metabolic Research Center)

**Status:** prospect, phase one estimate in progress
**Last updated:** 2026-09-02

## 1. Client name and what they do
Metabolic Research Center (MRC), a US weight-loss coaching company with a 40-year history. Multiple physical locations. Sells products and bundles online alongside its coaching programmes.

## 2. Current site
https://www.emetabolic.com/ — about 180 pages, built and held by a custom vendor platform that is failing them: qualified leads are declining month on month, and they cannot access useful analytics or ecommerce reporting.

## 3. Primary goal of the site
Lead generation first. The website is MRC's number one qualified-lead generator. Second, product sales, especially in November, their major promotional period.
