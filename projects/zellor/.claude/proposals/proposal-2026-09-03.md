## Scope, nine pages

Homepage, AI Shopping Assistant (with shoppable video section), Signals, Pricing,
Customers with case study template, Book a Demo, Contact, legal template, 404.

Product pages share one template. Case study is a CMS template so they can add more.

## Out of scope, phase two at €3,600

About, knowledge base landing and content pillar template, blog template,
"who it's for" pages, customer video gallery. Also out: copywriting, photography,
video sourcing.
