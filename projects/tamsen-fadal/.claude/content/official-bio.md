Tamsen Fadal is a multiple Emmy Award-winning journalist, filmmaker behind two  documtaries The (M) Factor and Before the Pause — and the instant New York Times bestselling author of How To Menopause. Her groundbreaking work is redefining one of the most overlooked chapters in a woman's life, midlife, and she's doing it across platforms with reach, impact, and authority — as a digital content creator with a following of millions across Instagram, Facebook, Threads, and YouTube.

She regularly advises healthcare startups, well brands, and global companies looking to better support women at work and beyond.

Tamsen is also the founder of #PostYourPatch, the viral campaign she created to remove the shame around menopause care, and has become a leading voice in the national advocacy effort around the estrogen patch shortage, including participating in an FDA listening session alongside Midi Health leadership.

TFM (Tamsen Fadal Media) produces award-winning content, national advocacy campaigns, live events, and educational tools designed to drive cultural change in how we talk about aging, health, and identity. Her work has been featured on The Today Show, Good Morning America, The Washington Post, and Forbes, and her films have been screened in more than 1,000 cities and 45 countries, reaching over 10 million viewers.

A former television news anchor and one of the few women in media to publicly share her experience with menopause, Tamsen is using her voice to build a movement, not just a message. In 2026, she was honored at the NYWICI Matrix Awards for her impact on the industry.

She is an investor in several wellness companies.
