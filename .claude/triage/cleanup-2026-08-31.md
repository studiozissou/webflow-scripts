## Recovery

The trashed batch sits at the top of Gmail's Trash (that view sorts by deletion
time). To find one specifically, search `in:trash` plus a sender or subject from the
tables above. Do **not** use `in:trash newer_than:1d` — Gmail's date operators filter
on arrival date, not deletion date, so two-week-old newsletters deleted seconds ago
won't match.
