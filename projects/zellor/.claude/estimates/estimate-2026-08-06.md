## Closing the gap

€8,120 against €5,000–6,000. Much closer than the rate card version, but still €2,120–3,120 over.
With CMS and components absorbed, every remaining lever is simply a page coming out.

| Lever | Saving | Note |
|-------|-------:|------|
| Solutions and Pricing as true placeholders | −480 | One hero and a CTA each at 240, rather than 480 |
| Defer the Customer Video Gallery | −480 | Depends on video they have not produced yet |
| Defer the Case Study template | −720 | They have no case studies written |
| Defer the Knowledge Base landing + pillar template | −1,200 | No content exists. The biggest single cut |

Any two of the middle three close the gap. But cutting is the worse story, because it
prices the site down rather than phasing it. Phasing keeps the full value visible.

## Flags

- **Investor meeting in a few weeks.** She floated launching a couple of pages by end of August. Worth pre-empting with a phase one that puts the homepage and product pages live first.
- **Timeline is content-blocked, not build-blocked.** Three to four weeks of build once Figma, copy, and images land. End of September is realistic.
- **Retainer opportunity.** She said "that's exactly what we need" to ongoing SEO and AEO management. €480/mo (4h) or €960/mo (8h). Worth putting in the proposal.
- **€680 for About Us** is the one figure off your €120/hr grid (5.67 hours). Every other number is a clean multiple. Worth a second look in case it should be 720.
- **Canva extras from her original brief** (brand guide, sales deck, social templates, video edit) are out of scope here and were never priced. Separate quote if she asks again.
