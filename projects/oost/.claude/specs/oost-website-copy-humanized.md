# Oost — website copy (humanized pass)
**Status:** Draft v2, after `/humanizer` · **Date:** 2026-09-13 · **Compare with:** `oost-website-copy.md` (v1, untouched)

Same structure, same keywords, same placeholders. What changed is the rhythm. v1 gave every dish the same shape (a description, then a clever four-word tag), closed every paragraph on a punchline, and reached for "not X but Y" too often. That reads as written-to-a-formula once you notice it. v2 lets some dishes be plain, lets a few sentences run on, and puts the jokes where a person would actually make them, which is about a third as often. A summary of the changes is at the bottom.

## What changed from v1, and why

**What made v1 read as generated**
- Every dish had the same two-beat shape: a description, then a four-word tag ("Hier zit de pit." "Mild en warm." "De basis onder alles."). Real menus are uneven.
- The same joke template three times: "het gerecht waar X om vragen / voor terugkomen / ons van moet kennen".
- Nearly every paragraph closed on a punchline ("Dat is het hele idee." "Dat mag ook." "Voorzichtig."). One or two land; ten is a tic.
- Contrast constructions stacked up: "geen buffet, maar", "geen bijzaak", "goed naast, beter in plaats van", "geen keten en geen foodhall".
- Triplets everywhere: "geduld, sambal en recepten", "zoet, rokerig, plakkerig", "kom eten, haal af, of laat ons".
- Everything was tidy. No sentence ran on, nothing was slightly too specific, no aside.

**What v2 does instead**
- Some dishes get one plain sentence. Two or three get a real detail (which fish, where oma is from, how many layers) and those are in brackets so the family fills in truth rather than us inventing it.
- The joke template is used once (babi ketjap), the others are replaced with something specific or nothing.
- Closing lines are kept where a person would actually say them (the 404, "Dan is hij op.") and cut elsewhere.
- Contrasts reduced to the two that carry meaning (rijsttafel is a Dutch-Indies custom; not a chain, not a foodhall).
- A few sentences are allowed to ramble the way people do when they're explaining their own restaurant ("en er toch altijd iemand is die nog een derde keer gaat").
- Triplets broken up or extended to four where the list is genuinely four things.
- Meta descriptions, titles, H1s, FAQs and the keyword map are unchanged; the SEO surface is identical.
