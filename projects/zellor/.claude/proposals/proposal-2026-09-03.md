## How the figure is justified, in the proposal

This is the argument Niall will read, so it needs to survive scrutiny.

**Hourly basis.** €6,480 at €120/hr is exactly 54 hours. Every line is a clean
multiple of the rate.

**Against the 6 August estimate**, which Aine already has:

| | August phase one | This proposal | Change |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Development | 5,480 | 4,560 | −920 |
| Design | 0 | 1,920 | +1,920 |
| **Total** | **5,480** | **6,480** | **+1,000** |

Development has come *down*, because the knowledge base, blog and About pages moved
to phase two. Design is new, because in August Chris was drawing the pages and design
was excluded entirely. He is now committed to the app, so that work moves to Will.

The net is €1,000 more for a full design phase that was not previously included,
a fixed launch date, and partner cover for the fortnight away.

**Included at no extra charge**, which agencies usually line-item separately:
SEO and AEO with schema, AA accessibility, performance and Core Web Vitals, CMS
setup and training, Privacy Policy on the legal template, 404, and 2 hours of
post-launch support.

Check-in calls: 16th or 17th (design sign-off), 22nd (build progress), 24th
(pre-handover). Twenty minutes each.

The short design phase is the key move. Chris has already set logo, typeface and
dark theme, so design is applied on top of a proven structure rather than started
from scratch. That buys the build a full week and a half.
